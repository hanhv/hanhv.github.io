About

I am an Assistant Professor of Practice in Mathematics at Purdue University. My research is in hyperbolic geometry and low-dimensional topology, with a particular interest in curves on surfaces.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral scholar at Arizona State University. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the University of Luxembourg, where my advisor was Hugo Parlier.
